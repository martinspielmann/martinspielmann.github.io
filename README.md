# Hi there, I'm Martin

I work as a Senior Cyber Security Engineer at KION Group and am Co-Founder & CTO at [MainDefense GmbH](https://maindefense.de).

My day-to-day work sits between incident response and systems architecture: investigating incidents, connecting detection to decision, and building the platforms that do the heavy lifting.

### What I work on

- **Agentic response & security platforms** - Architecting autonomous analysis and incident response systems, focused on the harnesses and guardrails that make them safe to run.
- **Incident response & forensics** - Active incident handling (SANS FOR508), detection engineering, and turning post-mortems into actual engineering fixes.
- **IT & OT deception** - Leading product architecture at [MainDefense](https://maindefense.de) for our deception platform DeepSea and PhishEye.
- **Pragmatic governance** - Translating technical risk for leadership and building a modern, AI-enabled ISO 27001 ISMS.
